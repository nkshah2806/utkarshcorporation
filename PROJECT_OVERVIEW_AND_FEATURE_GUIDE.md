# Uttkarsh Quantum Health Platform

## Complete Project Overview & Feature Guide

**Document purpose:** This guide explains the entire Uttkarsh Quantum Health Platform in simple, everyday language — no technical terms. It is written so that business owners, franchise partners, new team members, and clients can read it once and clearly explain the project to anyone else.

**How this guide was created:** Every feature described in this guide was verified by examining the actual project — the public website, the member portal, the admin portal, and the central data store. Nothing has been invented. Where a feature is only partially built, it is clearly marked as **"Partially Implemented"** so that no one mistakes it for a finished feature.

---

### 1. Executive Summary

The Uttkarsh Quantum Health Platform is a complete business software system built for a wellness and Ayurvedic health business. It helps the company run its "Quantum Machine Health Analysis" service from start to finish.

The platform works in three connected areas:

1. **A public website** where visitors can learn about the company, browse wellness products, view health camps, and apply to become a franchise member.
2. **A member portal** (used by franchise partners) where members register their clients, record health-scan readings, review the automatically prepared wellness guidance, and produce a final client report that can be printed as a PDF or shared on WhatsApp.
3. **An admin portal** (used by the head office) where administrators approve new members, manage all health information used in reports, control what appears on the public website, and watch business performance on dashboards.

All three areas share the same central information store, so a client registered by a member is instantly available to that member, and a report generated by a member can be seen in the admin's business dashboard.

The heart of the system is the **client report**: a professional, branded, bilingual (English/Hindi) report that explains a client's health readings, highlights normal and abnormal values, gives wellness guidance, recommends medicines from the company's medicine list, and includes a disclaimer — all in a format the member's own store branding is shown on.

### 4. Complete User Journey

This is the full journey of a client's report from start to finish, as the system is designed to be used:

**Step 1 — Member joins.** A person who wants to become a franchise member registers on the member portal with their name, email, phone, and password.

**Step 2 — Member completes their business profile.** After registering, the member fills in a detailed profile: their store name, state/city/district, franchise type, contact details, and their bank account information (account holder name, bank name, account number, account type, IFSC code, branch address). The system calculates how complete the profile is and shows a percentage.

**Step 3 — Member submits for approval and the head office approves.** The member submits the completed profile. The administrator reviews it and either approves or rejects it (if rejected, the member is told the reason). Only after approval can the member start doing client scans. The member can log in even before approval, but client/report features stay locked.

**Step 4 — Member registers a client.** The member adds the client's basic details — name, age, gender, mobile number, date of birth, weight, height, email, address. The system gives every client a unique client code (e.g., P0001).

**Step 5 — Member starts a scan (visit).** The member chooses the scan price from the company's price list (the system suggests the default price). The chosen price is locked to that scan so later price changes never affect past records.

**Step 6 — Member enters the scan readings.** The member opens the data-entry screen for that scan and enters the measured values for the health parameters. Values can be typed in one by one, or pasted as a list (CSV import). Parameters can be filtered by category and searched by name.

**Step 7 — System analyses the readings.** Once values are saved, the system compares every reading against the normal range stored for that parameter and marks it **Normal**, **Low**, or **High**. Based on the abnormal readings, the system automatically prepares wellness guidance sections (what the value means, possible causes, precautions, diet guidance, and medicine suggestions).

**Step 8 — Member reviews the report content.** In the Report Review Studio, the member sees every guidance point the system prepared. The member can tick or untick each point, select all or clear all, restore the system's original suggestions, choose which medicines to include (and in what dosage), add a note, and confirm the report price and payment status (paid/unpaid, payment method, transaction ID).

**Step 9 — Final report is generated.** The member clicks "Generate Final Report." The system compiles a complete, professionally designed report in the chosen language (English or Hindi) with the member's store branding, client details, all evaluated parameters with Normal/Low/High results, the selected guidance points, the selected medicines, the consultant's note, the next visit date, the report price, and the disclaimer. The scan is then marked as "Shared."

**Step 10 — Report is delivered.** The member can print the report, save it as a PDF (via the browser's print-to-PDF), or use the WhatsApp share button, which opens a chat with the client's phone number containing a ready message — the member attaches the PDF and sends it.

**Step 11 — Follow-up.** The member can set a next visit date. The dashboard reminds members about upcoming and overdue follow-ups.

### 6. Dashboard

The dashboard is the business cockpit for both administrators and members. Because members only see their own clients, the numbers a member sees are for their own business, while administrators see the whole company's numbers.

**What the dashboard shows:**

- **Counts:** Total patients/clients, patients added today/this week/this month, visits (scans) created, and reports generated
- **Trends:** A 7-day chart and a 6-month chart showing patient registrations and report generation over time
- **Result distribution:** A chart showing how many measured readings across all scans are Normal, Low, or High
- **Recent activity:** Recently registered patients and recently generated reports
- **Pending work:** Scans that still need a final report
- **Follow-ups:** Upcoming follow-up visits and overdue follow-ups, so no client is forgotten

### 10. Wellness / Quantum Assessment

This is the core engine of the platform. Here is how the assessment works in plain language:

**What is measured:** Each scan involves a set of health parameters (e.g., temperature, blood pressure, hemoglobin, blood sugar, and many more). The company stores, for every parameter:
- Its name (in English and Hindi)
- Its unit of measurement
- Its normal range (minimum and maximum)
- Its category (so parameters can be grouped and filtered)
- A full library of wellness guidance content written in English and Hindi

**How a reading is judged:** When a member enters a measured value, the system compares it to the normal range and instantly marks it:
- **Normal** (inside the range)
- **Low** (below the range)
- **High** (above the range)

**What the system prepares automatically:** For every abnormal reading, the system pulls together ready-made guidance sections, such as:
- What the finding means (report/explanation)
- The likely problem or concern
- Possible causes
- Precautions to take
- Diet guidance (what to eat / what to avoid)
- Recommended medicines from the medicine library

**The role of the consultant/member:** The member is the final decision maker. They review every suggestion in the Report Review Studio and decide what actually appears in the client's report. The system suggests; the member decides.

**History and safety:** Every time the company edits a parameter's guidance, the previous version is kept in that parameter's history. Also, the guidance that a member actually selects for a specific scan is saved with that scan, so a report never changes after it is finalised — even if the company later edits the master information.

### 11. Report Content Review Studio

This screen is where the member assembles the final report. It shows the scan's evaluated parameters and, for each one, the guidance points the system prepared.

**What the member can do here:**

- **Choose guidance points:** tick or untick any guidance point for any parameter, so only the points the member wants appear in the report
- **Select all / Deselect all:** quickly include or exclude everything
- **Reset:** restore the system's original default suggestions
- **Expand/collapse:** view one parameter at a time or expand all
- **Choose medicines:** select medicines from the company medicine library to include in the report, with details and dosage. Medicines can also be chosen per parameter
- **Add a medicine note:** a general note about medicines
- **Confirm report price & payment:** set the report amount and mark whether it is paid or unpaid (and if paid, by which method and transaction ID). This amount is locked into the report
- **Set next visit date:** schedule the follow-up so the dashboard can remind about it
- **Live preview:** see exactly which points are selected before generating

When the member is satisfied, they click **"Generate Final Report."** The scan is then marked as **Shared**, meaning a report has been produced for it.

### 12. Medicine Management

The company maintains a central medicine library that members can recommend inside reports.

**Medicine record:** Each medicine has a name, description/details, and dosage instructions.

**How it is used in reports:**
- Members select medicines in the Report Review Studio — either generally for the whole report or specifically for an individual parameter
- When a medicine is chosen for a scan, its name, details, and dosage are captured (snapshotted) onto that scan, so the printed report always shows exactly what was recommended at that time
- Later changes to the medicine library never alter reports that were already finalised

**Safety when deleting:** If a medicine is already used in any report, the system refuses to permanently delete it and instead simply deactivates it. This protects past reports while hiding the medicine from new selections.

### 13. Wellness / Ayurvedic Information

The wellness content in this system is written in the language of Ayurveda and natural wellness. Every parameter's guidance content is stored in two languages — English and Hindi — and the member chooses which language the final report is produced in.

**Types of wellness content the system understands and organises:**

| Content type | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Report / Explanation** | A plain-language description of what the reading means |
| **Problem** | The concern associated with an abnormal reading |
| **Cause** | What may be causing the abnormal value |
| **Precaution** | Steps the client should be careful about |
| **Diet (Pathya)** | What the client should eat — food guidance in the Ayurvedic tradition |
| **Avoid / Parhej** | What the client should avoid or cut down on |
| **Medicine** | Recommended medicines from the company's medicine library, with dosage |

**How the content is organised:** Beyond the simple content types above, the system also stores wellness guidance as a structured set of headings and points — a section, a heading, a bullet point, a sub-point, a paragraph, a recommendation, a precaution, and diet guidance. This structure is what lets the Report Review Studio show each guidance point as a separate item that the member can tick or untick independently.

**Both languages always available:** Every guidance item exists in English and Hindi. The member picks the report language at generation time, and the report is produced in that language. The stored guidance content is the same; only the language of the output changes.

**Safety of the final report:** Whatever the member finally selects for a scan is saved with that scan. Even if the head office later edits the wellness information, an already-finalised report keeps the exact guidance that was selected at the time.

### 14. Final Report

The final report is the main deliverable of the whole platform — the document the client receives. It is produced as a single, professionally designed document in the member's chosen language (English or Hindi).

**What the final report contains:**

- **Member store branding** — the member's store name and details appear on the report
- **Client details** — name, age, gender, mobile, date of birth, weight, height, address
- **Scan details** — the scan date, the scan price, and a next-visit date if set
- **Evaluated parameters** — every measured parameter with its value, unit, normal range, and a clear Normal / Low / High result
- **Selected guidance points** — only the guidance points the member ticked in the Report Review Studio (what the finding means, causes, precautions, diet, avoidance)
- **Selected medicines** — the medicines the member chose, with details and dosage (both general medicines and medicines chosen for a specific parameter)
- **Consultant's note** — a note the member added (if any)
- **Report price and payment status** — the amount and whether it was paid or unpaid, and if paid, the method and transaction ID
- **Disclaimer** — the company's disclaimer text, in the report's language

**Branding and consistency:** The report is compiled from the exact content the member selected, the exact medicines chosen, and the exact price confirmed. All of it is locked to that scan, so the printed report can never change later. A report is never generated with un-reviewed or unselected content.

### 15. PDF Generation & Sharing

Once the final report is ready, the member has three ways to deliver it:

**1. Print directly.** The member can open the print view of the report. The report is designed on A4 pages with a clean layout, so it prints neatly on any standard printer.

**2. Save as PDF.** From the same print view, the member uses the browser's standard "Save as PDF" option to store the report as a PDF file on their computer or phone. No extra software is needed.

**3. Share on WhatsApp.** The report screen has a WhatsApp share button. When pressed, it opens a WhatsApp chat with the client's phone number and a pre-written message is placed in the chat box (the message includes the client's name). The member then attaches the saved PDF file to the message and sends it.

**Important note:** The system does not automatically attach the file to the WhatsApp message — the member saves the PDF and attaches it manually. This is how sharing works today.

### 16. Report History

Every time a final report is generated, the system keeps a permanent record of it. This gives both members and the head office a complete history of what was delivered.

**What is recorded for each report:**

- The client and the scan it belongs to
- The language the report was produced in (English or Hindi)
- Who generated the report and when
- The report amount and payment status
- A full snapshot of the report content — the selected guidance, medicines, price, next visit date, and disclaimer — so the record matches exactly what the client received

**How reports appear in history:**

- A report history list shows all generated reports with their details
- The dashboard counts reports generated today/this week/this month and over the last 6 months
- Scans that have a generated report are marked as **Shared**; scans still awaiting a report show as **Report Ready** (data entered, report not yet finalised)

**Why this matters:** Because each report keeps its own snapshot, even if the company later changes the master wellness information, medicine library, prices, or disclaimers, old reports and old records stay exactly as they were delivered.

### 20. Account & Security

The platform has a complete account system for administrators and members.

**Registration and login:**

- Members register with their full name, email, phone, and password
- Login is done with email/username and password
- Members can use the **Forgot Password** flow to reset their password (the system guides them through setting a new one)

- Approve or reject member profiles

- Passwords are stored in a protected form so they are never visible in the ordinary member list

**Member self-service:**

- Members can update their own account details
- Members can choose their preferred language (English / Hindi / Gujarati)
- Members can change their portal password from their profile

### 21. Access Control (Who Can See What)

The system carefully controls what each person can see and do. This is one of its most important safety features.

| Person | What they can see and do |

- This separation is enforced at the central information store level, so it cannot be bypassed by a member from any screen

### 22. Complete Data Flow (Plain-Language Walkthrough)

Here is how information moves through the platform, step by step, in everyday language:

**1. Head office prepares the foundation.** The administrator defines the health parameters and their normal ranges, writes the wellness guidance in English and Hindi, builds the medicine library, sets the scan prices, writes the disclaimer, and publishes website content, health camps, and legal pages. All of this lives in one shared central store.

**2. A member joins and is approved.** The member registers, completes their business and bank profile, and the head office approves them. From this moment the member is a recognised business partner in the system.

**3. The member registers a client.** The member enters the client's details. The central store creates the client record under that member's name, so the client can only be seen by that member (and the head office).

**4. The member starts a scan and chooses the price.** The member picks the scan price from the active price list. The price is copied onto the scan so later price changes never affect it.

**5. The member enters the readings.** The measured values are saved for the scan. The system immediately compares each value with the parameter's normal range and marks it Normal, Low, or High.

**6. The system prepares the guidance.** For every abnormal reading, the system pulls the relevant wellness guidance (explanation, problem, cause, precaution, diet, avoidance, medicines) from the stored content.

**7. The member reviews and selects.** In the Report Review Studio, the member ticks the guidance points they want, selects medicines, adds a note, sets the price and payment, and schedules the next visit. Only what the member selects is saved for the report.

**8. The final report is generated and locked.** The system compiles the branded report in the chosen language, snapshots everything onto the scan (content, medicines, price, next visit date, disclaimer), and marks the scan as Shared. From this point the report cannot change.

**9. The report is delivered.** The member prints it, saves it as a PDF, and shares it on WhatsApp with the client.

**10. The business sees everything.** The dashboards read the same central store and show counts, trends, result distributions, pending work, and follow-up reminders — the member sees their own numbers, the head office sees the whole company.

### 24. Real-World Example

To make everything concrete, here is one complete real-world example of how the system is used:

**The scene:** A franchise member, Mr. Sharma, runs a wellness store in Jaipur. He has been approved by the head office.

**Morning:** Mrs. Gupta visits the store for a health scan. Mr. Sharma opens his member portal, goes to Patients, and registers her — name, age, mobile, address. The system gives her client code **P0027**.

**Later:** The dashboard reminds Mr. Sharma that Mrs. Gupta's follow-up is due in two weeks. The head office, meanwhile, sees the new report in the company-wide dashboard and the result distribution across all scans.

### 30. One-Minute Project Explanation

**The Uttkarsh Quantum Health Platform is a complete software system for a wellness and Ayurvedic health business.**

The company sells "Quantum Machine Health Analysis" — a service where a franchise member scans a client, and the client receives a detailed wellness report. The software manages that entire business from start to finish.

**It has three connected parts:**

1. **A public website** where people learn about the company, browse wellness products, see health camps, and apply to become franchise members.
2. **A member portal** for the franchise partners. Members register their clients, enter the scan readings, and the system instantly checks every reading against normal ranges and marks it Normal, Low, or High. For anything abnormal, it automatically prepares wellness guidance — what the finding means, possible causes, precautions, diet advice, and suggested medicines.
3. **An admin portal** for the head office. Administrators approve new members, maintain all the health information and medicine lists used in reports, set scan prices, manage website content, create health camps, write the privacy policy and terms, and watch the whole business on dashboards.

**The heart of the system is the client report.** In the Report Review Studio, the member picks exactly which guidance points go into the report, chooses medicines, confirms the price and payment, and then generates a professional, branded report in English or Hindi. The report can be printed, saved as a PDF, or shared on WhatsApp. Everything in a finished report is locked to that report — later changes to prices, medicines, or guidance never change a report already delivered.

**Safety is built in.** Members only ever see their own clients — the head office sees everything. A member cannot work until the head office approves their profile. Old reports are preserved permanently.

**In one sentence:** The platform turns a health machine's raw readings into a professional, branded, bilingual wellness report — and runs the whole franchise business around it, from member approval to client registration to final delivery on WhatsApp.
